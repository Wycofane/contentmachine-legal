Creator video workspace

From a recorded moment to a finished clip.

ContentMachine brings highlight review, vertical video editing and social post preparation into one browser workspace, running on a self-hosted server.

Review the moment.

Preview saved highlights, compare suggested clips and keep editorial control. Audio and transcript signals help sort candidates; scores are estimates, not predictions of audience reach.

Shape the story.

Trim footage, reframe gameplay and facecam, work with multiple recorded perspectives, and prepare vertical videos with editable captions and hook text.

Prepare the post.

Create editable platform-specific titles, captions and hashtags. Review your export and choose a connected destination account before initiating an upload.

A workflow with the creator in control.

  1. Choose authorized footage. Use videos you created or have permission to edit and share. Public availability alone does not grant reuse rights.
  2. Review and edit. Inspect the suggested highlight, adjust the cut and layout, correct captions and watch the finished export.
  3. Connect an account. Supported integrations use the platform's authorization screen. ContentMachine does not ask for your social account password.
  4. Choose where the video goes. Select the destination and initiate the transfer yourself. Upload progress and platform responses appear in the workspace.

TikTok integration

The initial TikTok workflow is an upload to the creator's TikTok inbox through the Content Posting API. After a successful transfer, the creator opens TikTok's inbox notification, continues editing, chooses the final post settings and completes publication in TikTok. Uploading a file is not the same as publishing it.

Connecting TikTok also supports identifying the authorized account and displaying its permitted profile statistics and recent-video view totals in the workspace. Access depends on the scopes granted by TikTok and the account holder. ContentMachine is independent of TikTok and is not endorsed by it.
